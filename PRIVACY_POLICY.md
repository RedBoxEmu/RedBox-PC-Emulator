# Privacy Policy for RedBox PC Emulator

Last updated: October 2, 2026

RedBox PC Emulator is developed by Rimvydop.

## Information We Collect

RedBox PC Emulator does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The app is designed to run virtual machines locally on the user's device.

## Files and Storage

RedBox may allow users to select files such as disk images, ISO images, and other virtual machine files from their device.

These files are used locally for virtual machine functionality. RedBox does not upload these files to Rimvydop servers.

## Network Access

Virtual machines running through RedBox may use network functionality when networking is enabled by the user.

Network activity performed inside a virtual machine depends on the operating system, software, and websites used inside that virtual machine.

## Third-Party Services

RedBox may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play and GitHub. Those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

RedBox is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RedBox features or privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
